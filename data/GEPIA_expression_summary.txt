GEPIA3 Gene Expression and Survival Analysis
============================================

Project
-------
Comprehensive Bioinformatics Analysis of the Human PTEN Gene and Protein

Database
--------
GEPIA3 (Gene Expression Profiling Interactive Analysis)

Data Sources
------------
• The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA)
• Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx)

Gene Analyzed
-------------
PTEN (Phosphatase and Tensin Homolog)

Expression Analysis
-------------------
PTEN expression was analyzed across multiple human cancer types using GEPIA3. Differential expression analysis compared tumor tissues with corresponding normal tissues from TCGA and GTEx datasets.

Key Findings
------------
• PTEN expression varies among different cancer types.
• Differential expression patterns suggest tissue-specific regulation of PTEN.
• Altered PTEN expression is associated with tumor development and progression in several malignancies.
• Expression analysis supports the established role of PTEN as a major tumor suppressor gene.

Survival Analysis
-----------------
Kaplan–Meier survival analysis was performed using GEPIA3 to evaluate the prognostic significance of PTEN expression.

Observations
------------
• PTEN expression showed prognostic significance in selected cancer types.
• The impact of PTEN expression on overall survival differs depending on the tumor type.
• These findings suggest that PTEN expression may serve as a potential prognostic biomarker in specific cancers.

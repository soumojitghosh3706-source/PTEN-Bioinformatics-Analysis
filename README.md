# Comprehensive Bioinformatics Analysis of the Human PTEN Gene and Protein

An independent **in silico bioinformatics project** investigating the human **PTEN (Phosphatase and Tensin Homolog)** gene and protein using publicly available biological databases and computational tools.

---

## Overview

PTEN is one of the most important tumour suppressor genes in humans and plays a central role in regulating the PI3K/AKT signalling pathway. This project presents a comprehensive bioinformatics investigation of PTEN, integrating genomic, proteomic, structural, functional, and clinical analyses using multiple online databases and computational tools.

---

## Project Objectives

- Retrieve and annotate the PTEN gene.
- Analyse genomic organization and transcript information.
- Characterize the PTEN protein sequence and physicochemical properties.
- Identify conserved domains and structural features.
- Investigate protein–protein interaction networks.
- Perform functional enrichment and pathway analyses.
- Explore disease-associated variants.
- Assess PTEN as a therapeutic target.
- Evaluate gene expression and survival significance across human cancers.

Primary Biological Function
---------------------------
PTEN is a dual-specificity phosphatase that negatively regulates the PI3K/AKT signalling pathway by converting phosphatidylinositol (3,4,5)-trisphosphate (PIP3) into phosphatidylinositol (4,5)-bisphosphate (PIP2). It plays an essential role in regulating cell proliferation, apoptosis, migration, metabolism, and tumour suppression.
